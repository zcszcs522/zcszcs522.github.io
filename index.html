About Me

I am a first-year Computer Science PhD student at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, advised by Prof. Francis Y. Yan.

I completed my undergraduate studies in Computer Science at the Institute for Interdisciplinary Information Sciences (IIIS, Yao Class) at Tsinghua University in June 2026. My undergraduate major GPA was 3.8/4.0.

My current research explores whether reinforcement learning can rediscover effective heuristics for networked systems, with a focus on understanding learned policies and diagnosing why training succeeds or fails. My previous work spans multi-agent reinforcement learning, vision-language models, and game theory.

Research Interests

  • Reinforcement learning for systems and networking
  • Agentic AI for systems
  • Systems for agentic AI
